# Front page (/)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: students and young researchers first, then legal, finance and business teams. Job: understand that DocuMind answers questions about their files with page citations while the files stay on their computer, then open the workspace. Action: Open the workspace (/workspace). Proof: live readouts from the visitor's own browser (WebGPU, free storage, model cache), the comparison ledger, the workspace preview walkthrough, the grounding playground, documented product facts. Constraints: no invented stats, customers or logos; claims about cloud tools must hold for cloud AI chat tools in general. The workspace keeps its pastel look for now.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A cyber-brutalist instrument page: DocuMind presented as a machine that checks your own browser live and shows its work, with the cloud-versus-local ledger as its argument. It refuses the soft SaaS hero and every invented status number.

OWN-WORLD: Light grey ground (#F1F1EF), near-black ink, one signal red (#D3110F) for labels, the cited line and live states; 1px hairline grids and black 1px frames, square corners, crosshair markers; Anton set huge and uppercase for display, Martian Mono for labels, readouts, buttons and body; numbered section labels /01 to /07.

STORY: The visitor sees the headline and watches their own browser being checked, reads the ledger of cloud chat against DocuMind, watches the preview answer with citations, reads the facts, tries a question, reads the answers, then opens the workspace.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Fixed nav with mono links split by slashes and a black Open workspace button. Left half: /01 label, a three-to-four line Anton headline, a red mono subline, two lines of body, the black primary button and a mono text link. Right half: a framed scan panel on a hairline grid with corner crosshairs: a document page drawn as text bars whose cited line is red, and a readout box listing this browser's WebGPU, free storage and model cache as they resolve.

FORM: User-pinned visual world (cyber brutalism in red) over the comparison-ledger structure, position 4 of 6 on the ordered list. seed:ad87db8e8d408a5d

Signature interaction: on load a red scan line sweeps the page and stops on the cited line while the readouts resolve from real browser checks; the ledger stamps keep their single press.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
