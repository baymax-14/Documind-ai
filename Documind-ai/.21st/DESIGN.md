# DocuMind AI workspace design context

A research and student document workbench in React and Vite.

## Product identity
- Creative direction: The Paper Workbench, Neo-Brutalist Pastel Harmony.
- Surface: warm cream `#FFFDF5`; structure and text: black `#000000`.
- Accents: mint `#88D498`, sky `#74B9FF`, butter `#FFD23F`.
- Typography: Space Grotesk for headings and DM Sans for interface text.
- Edges: bold black borders, square or 4px corners, hard offset shadows.

## Workspace constraints
- One main job: select local files and find examples in the current workspace.
- The seeded documents are demo examples. Selected files add only names and metadata to the page session.
- Upload, persistence, parsing, AI answers and citations are not connected.
- Preserve the shared DocuMind AI brand component and existing Lucide icon style.
- Responsive breakpoints, visible keyboard focus and reduced motion are required.
